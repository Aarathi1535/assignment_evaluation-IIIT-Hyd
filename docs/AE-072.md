# [AE-072] Reconcile QR vs OMR vs Manual Identification Precedence

## Objective

Define and enforce one authoritative student identity by establishing QR-over-OMR precedence for automated identification while allowing manual/operator identification to override any automated result.

## Identification precedence

Identification is governed by two rules:

1. **Among automated sources: QR > OMR**
   - A valid QR identification takes precedence over an OMR identification.
   - OMR is used when QR does not produce a usable student ID.
   - Conflicting QR and OMR results must be recorded rather than silently discarded.

2. **Manual identification overrides automation**
   - An operator can override a successful QR or OMR identification at any time.
   - Manual identification becomes the authoritative identity.
   - The previous automated identification must remain available in history.
   - Manual identification is also the only path when both QR and OMR fail or are ambiguous.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Automated identification precedence is explicitly enforced as QR > OMR.
- [ ] A valid QR identification takes precedence over a valid OMR identification.
- [ ] OMR is used when QR does not produce a usable student ID.
- [ ] Manual/operator identification can override any automated QR or OMR result.
- [ ] Manual identification is available when both QR and OMR fail or are ambiguous.
- [ ] Previous automated identification remains available in history after a manual override.
- [ ] Conflicting QR and OMR results are recorded for diagnosis.
- [ ] Only one student ID is persisted as the authoritative identity for an `AnswerScript`.
- [ ] Page-level QR/OMR detection results are promoted to the assembled `AnswerScript` identity.
- [ ] Reprocessing a page does not create competing authoritative student identities.
- [ ] `IdentificationSource` distinguishes `QR`, `OMR`, `OPERATOR`, and existing `OCR` sources.
- [ ] OMR is not represented using the existing `OCR` source.
- [ ] The authoritative identification source is persisted with the `AnswerScript`.
- [ ] Existing identification history is preserved when the authoritative identity changes.

## Validation

- [ ] Test valid QR + valid OMR and verify QR becomes authoritative.
- [ ] Test failed QR + valid OMR and verify OMR becomes authoritative.
- [ ] Test valid QR + valid OMR with conflicting student IDs and verify QR remains authoritative while the conflict is recorded.
- [ ] Test failed/ambiguous QR + failed/ambiguous OMR and verify the manual identification path is available.
- [ ] Test manual override of a successful QR identification and verify the manual result becomes authoritative.
- [ ] Test manual override of a successful OMR identification and verify the manual result becomes authoritative.
- [ ] Verify the previous automated identification remains available in history after manual override.
- [ ] Verify authoritative identity belongs to `AnswerScript`, not independently competing page identities.
- [ ] Verify reprocessing does not create multiple authoritative student IDs.
- [ ] Verify `IdentificationSource.OMR` is distinct from `IdentificationSource.OCR`.
