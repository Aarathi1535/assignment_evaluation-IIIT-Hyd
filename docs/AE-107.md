# AE-107 — Professor Dashboard: Overall Exam Grading Summary

### Objective

Extend the dashboard with an overall exam-level grading summary.

---

### What we're building

Overall exam status.

---

### Done when

- Display total graded count.

- Display overall grading percentage.

- Display aggregate progress across all TAs.

- Display a naive ETA based on reliable grading completion timestamps.

- ETA must use `completedAt` data and must not use `updatedAt` as a completion-time proxy.

### Anti-invention clause

> **Strict Semantic Boundary:** Do not infer grading progress, completion time, ETA, or grading duration from fields whose semantics do not guarantee those values.

AE-106A must provide `completedAt` before the ETA criterion is implemented.
